# Graduate-cohort and fee verification — 2026-09-10

## Verified nationwide graduate reference

- Publisher: Jordan Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research.
- Statistics index: https://www.mohe.gov.jo/Ar/List/الإحصاءات
- Document: https://www.mohe.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/ar/eb_list_page/خلاصة_بالطلبة_الخريجين2022.pdf
- Exact index title: «خلاصة إحصائية بأعداد الطلبة الخريجين للعام الجامعي 2022- 2023».
- The PDF's printed academic year is **2022/2023**. The filename ending in 2022 is not a 2022-only reference period.
- The PDF Grand Total is **74,718** across its included institutions and qualification levels. This includes diploma levels; do not label the whole total bachelor's graduates.
- Bachelor's graduates: **56,781**.
- Other printed totals: doctorate 1,050; master's 7,318; technical diploma 95; higher diploma 2,430; intermediate diploma 7,001; professional diploma 39; higher specialization 4. These sum to 74,718 with bachelor's.
- Population is graduates in the institutions listed in the official table; no nationality restriction is identified in the document. Do not relabel as Jordanian-national graduates only.
- Verification: parsed official PDF text; downloaded the original; rendered and visually inspected page 1, including headings and the Grand Total row.
- Evidence: `mohe-graduates-2022-2023.pdf` and `mohe-graduates-2022-2023.png` in this directory.
- The ministry index retrieved on 2026-09-10 lists 2022–2023 first and 2021–2022 next. Its displayed website last-modified date 2026/09/06 does **not** update the cohort year.
- A bounded official-domain search for 2023/2024 and 2024/2025 nationwide graduate summaries did not identify a newer comparable aggregate. This is the latest comparable graduate summary found in that index, not a proof that no newer figure exists anywhere.
- Recommended product label: «خريجو البكالوريوس — 2022/2023» with value 56,781 and linked source; contextual note «أحدث خلاصة خريجين منشورة في فهرس الوزارة الذي تحققنا منه في 10 أيلول 2026».
- Recommended data status: verified dated snapshot. Never describe these counts as the 2026 graduate cohort or newly live statistics.

## Credit-fee interpretation — directly verified source methodology

- **Resolved:** https://www.admhec.gov.jo/AboutPage.aspx, section «قسم تخصصات الجامعات», explicitly says that this section includes «رسوم الساعات، وفروع الثانوية المسموحة».
- This first-party description directly supports treating the regular-fee column in the linked Majors catalog as the regular credit-hour fee. It applies to the catalog generally, so independent evidence for each university is unnecessary for the unit interpretation.
- Verification performed through the live official About page on 2026-09-10. Keep its link in adapter/source documentation.
