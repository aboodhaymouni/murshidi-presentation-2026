Welcome
The Crown Prince Award for Best Government Services Application is a national competition designed to engage
students from public and private universities and community colleges across Jordan in shaping the future of digital
public services. Launched in 2019, the award has become a flagship initiative to foster innovation, stimulate
problem-solving, and empower young people to actively contribute to the development of high-quality, accessible
government services through mobile applications.

INTRODUCTION
Over the years, we have continuously refined our approach to outreach, mentorship, and evaluation, making the award
more inclusive, accessible, and supportive of students from all academic backgrounds. Recognizing the need for more
targeted guidance and practical support, awareness efforts have been expanded to reach more institutions, and
sector-specific insights have been introduced to help participants align their ideas with real service needs and enhance
citizens’ lives. These efforts are strategically aligned with the Economic Modernization Vision 2030 and broader national
policies, ensuring that the award contributes to Jordan’s long-term development goals.
Crown Prince Award
For Best Government Service Application The 2024 edition marked a significant milestone in the award’s evolution, engaging 352 students from 25 educational
institutions. The award’s sectoral coverage expanded to 11 domains, representing a %22.2 growth compared to 2023.
Submissions showcased clear advancements in innovation depth, technical feasibility, and user-centered design—results
credited to improved participant preparation and more structured guidance throughout the application process.
This manual was developed to guide students throughout their journey in the competition. It provides clear and structured information on how to
participate, including the eligibility criteria, evaluation criteria, award categories, timelines, and prizes. It also outlines the core values that guide the
award: transparency, fairness, inclusiveness, and innovation with real public impact.
Spearheaded by the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship, in collaboration with the Crown Prince Foundation and in partnership with
universities, community colleges, and stakeholders from both the public and private sectors, the award aims to empower a generation of creative
thinkers, digital innovators, and civic-minded problem-solvers. It encourages students to develop solutions that address challenges in government
services—making them more efficient, intelligent, and responsive to citizens’ needs.
By participating, students not only gain valuable experience in real-world innovation practices but also contribute to shaping Jordan’s digital future. We
encourage you to explore this manual thoroughly, leverage the available resources, and present your ideas with purpose, creativity, and ambition.
Step up. Make an impact. Reimagine public services. Let’s build the future together.

4. EVALUATION TEAMS
To uphold the principles of fairness, rigor, and transparency, the evaluation process of the Crown Prince Award for Best Government Services
Application follows a structured, multi-stage framework, led by specialized evaluation teams. This tiered review model ensures that each undergoes a
comprehensive assessment beginning with eligibility screening followed by evaluation of technical performance, and concluding with a review of
innovation and societal impact.
The CPA is more than an award—it is a national platform that empowers future leaders to demonstrate what possible when creativity meets
public sector challenges.
This approach not only strengthens objectivity and integrity of the process, but also reinforce the Award’s commitment to sector relevance, real-world
impact, and the scalability of student driven digital solutions.
i. Initial Screening Team
Managed by: Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship (MoDEE)
Purpose:
This team is responsible for conducting a comprehensive initial review of all submitted applications to ensure they fulfill the basic eligibility and
technical submission requirements prior to advancing to the formal evaluation stages.
Evaluation Dimensions:
The team is responsible for verifying compliance with all eligibility and submission criteria. This includes confirming student’s enrollment status,
validating team composition and ensuring that the application was developed in house by the student team. Additionally, submissions are reviewed
for completeness, proper file formatting, and bilingual functionality (Arabic and English).
Scoring Output:
Only submissions that fully meet the eligibility and submission criteria will advance to the technical evaluation stage. Entries that are incomplete or
non-compliant will be disqualified to uphold the integrity and fairness of the competition.
Each Applications will result in one of the following outcomes:
• Approved for progression to the technical review phase.
• Rejected due to ineligibility or failure to meet submission requirements.

AWARD CATEGORIES & RECOGNITION PATHWAYS
The Crown Prince Award for Best Government Services Application invites ambitious, innovative, and solution- driven students from public and
private Jordanian universities and community colleges to submit ideas that transform public service delivery through mobile technology.
Unlike traditional competitions that limit participation to specific themes, this award takes a broad, and inclusive approach— empowering students
to propose innovations across the entire spectrum of government services. Whether you're solving issues in healthcare, transportation, education,
environment, or civic services, your innovation is eligible and encouraged.
Your app doesn’t need to fit into a predefined box—it simply needs to address a meaningful challenge in how citizens connect with and experience
government services.
Recognition for the Top 10 Teams
Each year, 10 exceptional teams are selected as finalists based on their evaluation score and demonstrated innovation potential.
These teams represent a diverse range of sectors, challenges and Innovative solutions.
Track 1: Financial Award & Official Recognition (Top 5 Teams)
The top five scoring teams receive a monetary award and an official certificate of excellence issued by the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepre-
neurship (MoDEE), and The Crown Prince Foundation (CPF).
Award Distrbution:
▪ The first %25 of the award is disbursed as an upfront cash payment, while the remaining %75 is released in stages based on verified project progress.
▪ Award funds are distributed across agreed milestones (%25 ,%20 ,%30), according to a detailed plan established between each winning team and MoDEE.
▪ MoDEE provides ongoing guidance and technical oversight to support winners in meeting their milestones and maximize project impact.
This phased funding ensures that award resources directly support the development and potential implementation of winning applications,
encouraging continuity, accountability, and measurable real-world outcomes.

PARTICIPATION GUIDELINES & TIPS
To ensure a smooth experience and protect your team’s effort and ideas, please follow these key guidelines when participating in the Crown Prince
Award for Best Government Services Application. These tips highlight what you must know and do to stay eligible, fair, and competitive at every
stage.
1. Stay Eligible:
▪ Make sure all team members meet the official Eligibility Criteria described in this guide at the time you submit your application.
▪ Only students who are currently enrolled in an accredited public or private Jordanian university or community college (as of the Award launch date) can
participate.
▪ Never provide false or misleading information — any team found doing so will be disqualified immediately.
2. Keep It Original
▪ Your application must be the team’s own original work — do not copy or reuse code without proper credit.
▪ The mobile app must be developed entirely by the student team — hiring external developers or contractors is not allowed.
▪ Using open-source libraries or frameworks is fine, as long as you give proper credit and avoid plagiarism.
▪ Make sure your app works on modern mobile operating systems — outdated or unsupported versions won’t be accepted.
▪ Keep your submission accessible and functional during the entire evaluation period. Teams are responsible for making sure the app remains live and
testable.
3. One Final Submission
▪ Once you submit your application, it’s final — you can’t change or edit it after the deadline.
▪ The only exception is if you get written permission from the Award administrators to fix a technical error.

The HRH Crown Prince Best Government Application Award is made possible through the collaboration and support of several esteemed
partners. These partnerships are crucial in fostering innovation, supporting youth, and driving the development of exceptional government

Crown prince Foundation (CPF):
The award is organized annually by the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship in cooperation and partnership with the Crown

Public and private Universities and colleges:

organizing internal sessions with the students, universities actively encourage participation in the award. These sessions aim to motivate
students, provide guidance on the application process, and offer support in developing innovative solutions.
Private sector:
Private sector representatives are actively involved in the award's committees and evaluation process. Their participation ensures that the
evaluation criteria align with industry standards and that the selected solutions have practical, real-world applications. These representatives
bring valuable insights and expertise to the steering and evaluation committees, helping to assess the technical feasibility, innovation potential,
and market readiness of the applications.
